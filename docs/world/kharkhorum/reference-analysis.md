# Kharkhorum — Reference Image Analysis

The approved concept image (owner-supplied, 2026-10-06) is the **primary visual reference** for composition,
silhouette, scale relationships, district placement and atmosphere. It is a concept, not the world: SÜLD
builds an **original** city with the same *type* of composition. History (see
[../KHARKHORUM_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md](../KHARKHORUM_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md)) supplies the details.

## 1. Viewpoint and reading
- **Main panel**: a high oblique aerial view **from the south looking north**. The south wall and South Gate
  are in the foreground; the palace and snow-capped mountains are in the background.
- **Inset panels** show the visual targets per area: Imperial Palace, Central Plaza, Market, City Gate,
  Residential, Spiritual, Military, Palace Interior, River & Bridge, Night View, Gardens.

## 3. Structure of the composition
1. **One dominant north–south ceremonial axis.** South Gate → avenue → **Grand Bridge** over the canal →
   stairs → **Central Plaza + equestrian monument** → avenue → **grand stair** → **Imperial Palace**. Every
   major sightline ends on the palace.
2. **Radial secondary roads** leave the plaza toward the districts (west → residential, east → market,
   north-west → spiritual, north-east → military). There is a cross-axis through the plaza to the side gates.
3. **Layered elevation rising to the north.** Gardens and canal are lowest (−1…0), the plaza is raised (+1),
   the districts rise gently, the spiritual hill sits at +4, and the palace terrace at +8 tops the skyline.
4. **Visual hierarchy by height:** palace (≈ 30–34 tall) > gate towers and stupa (≈ 22–26) > military
   towers > market halls (8–14) > yurts (5–7).
5. **Density gradient.** The market is densest, residential is mid-density and organic (not a grid),
   gardens and the canal are open, and the palace forecourt is open and ceremonial.

## 5. Architectural language read from the image, and how SÜLD translates it
| In the image | SÜLD translation (original, Minecraft blocks) |
|---|---|
| Grey crenellated stone walls, red banners with gold emblems | Stone-brick and tuff walls on a **packed-mud base course** (a historical nod). Banners are woven from red wool with a gold SÜLD emblem in pixels: not copied heraldry, and no banner block entities needed. |
| Tiered dark roofs with up-turned eaves and gold ridges | **Deepslate-tile roofs** with stair eaves turned up at the corners and gold/yellow-terracotta ridges, used sparingly. Archaeology found Chinese-style roof tiles at Karakorum, so tiled halls are grounded. Proportions stay **low and broad** to avoid "generic East-Asian fantasy". |
| Red lacquered columns on stone plinths | **Red-terracotta and mangrove columns** on polished-andesite plinths. The throne hall uses an **8 × 8 column grid** (the 64-column palace of Ögedei). |
| White felt yurts with blue/orange trim | Yurts (gers) in white wool, a sky-blue band, an orange door and a crown slab. **Several sizes**, organically placed. |
| Equestrian statue on a stepped pedestal | **Хааны Морьт Хөшөө**: an original SÜLD rider monument (voxel now; a Meshy → Blender → Blockbench model later). |
| Stupa with prayer-flag lines | White **suburgan** with a gold spire, plus **prayer-flag strings** of coloured wool between poles. |
| Awning stalls, lanterns, crates, carts | Market stalls with **3+ awning variants**, barrels, crates, hay and carts. Lanterns on posts. |
| Night: warm lantern glow everywhere | Lanterns every 8–10 blocks on main roads; braziers (campfires) on towers and gates. |

**Avoid:** European castle keeps and spires, Japanese torii and pagoda stacks, oversized everything.

## 6. Atmosphere targets
Day: sunlit, golden roofs, green gardens, blossom accents. Night: dark sky, warm lantern chains along the axis,
fires on the gate and tower beacons. Dark-fantasy: weathered stone, soot near the forges, and ominous
banners at the military compound.

## 7. What the first slice must prove (§25 of the directive)
A walkable path along the axis **South Gate → avenue + Grand Bridge → Central Plaza + monument → grand stair
→ palace entrance**, plus:
- one market street off the plaza's east side
- one yurt block west
- one blacksmith
- one shrine
- one watchtower

All of it in the reference's visual language, built as real blocks, validated and rendered from the
real world.
