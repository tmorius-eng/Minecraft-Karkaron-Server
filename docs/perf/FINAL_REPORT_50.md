# Final report: 50 players, 10 000-block world (overnight 2026-10-07/08)

This is the 26-point report the performance brief asks for. It covers what was measured, what changed and where the
evidence is. The details are in [WORLD_50.md](WORLD_50.md), [../world/WORLD_BORDER.md](../world/WORLD_BORDER.md) and
[../world/PREGENERATION.md](../world/PREGENERATION.md).

The bench is a shared, virtualised 4-core Xeon at 2.8 GHz, and the bots run on the same cores. Its numbers are the
pessimistic end. **The release gate is not passed on this bench** (point 25).

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **World border** | Native vanilla border, 10 000 × 10 000 (radius 5 000), centred on the spawn (−16, −46, Kharkhorum's plaza). It is set once in level.dat by `WorldBorderService`. Warning distance 24, damage buffer 4, 0.5/block. Teleports outside the border are cancelled (`suld.border.bypass` is op-only). It persists across restarts and the spawn is inside. Config: `world.border.*`. |
| 2 | **View distance** | **6** (`deploy/templates/server.properties.tpl`). It was measured against 7 and 8. |
| 3 | **Simulation distance** | **4**. It was measured against 5 and 6. |
| 4 | **Chunk and entity settings** | In `deploy/paper-tuning.conf`, applied by `deploy.sh` through `paper_tune.py`, which refuses unknown keys: player chunk-generate rate 40/s; monster cap 30, spawn attempts every 10 ticks; ambient (bat) cap 4 and water ambient cap 8, each attempted every 400 ticks; monster despawn soft/hard 30/72; item merge radius 2.0. |
| 5 | **Pre-generation strategy** | SÜLD's own throttled pre-generator (Chunky is optional). Priority areas only: P0 spawn and city (384 blocks), P1 routes to the inner areas, P2 shrines and dungeon grounds, P3 the 24 area hearts. P4 outer routes and P5 the whole border are off by default. The schedule is a closed-form square spiral with no overlaps. Requests go through `getChunkAtAsync` at most 6 per tick and 12 in flight, with an allowance that adapts to MSPT. Progress persists to `pregen.yml` and the run is resumable. |
| 6 | **Generated chunks** | Plan: 10 311 priority chunks, all done. The dev world now holds **112 040 / 390 625** chunks (28.7 %; 166 region files, 789 MB), most from exploring bots. Generating everything would take about 2 GB (7 KB per chunk). |
| 7 | **Entity counts** | 50 players: 5 266 (F50) to 7 132 (E50) entities. The largest groups are creepers, zombies and skeletons (500–600 each, before the monster cap of 30), dropped items (about 650) and generated passive animals. With no players: 8. SÜLD keeps no entity alive after logout (soak). |
| 8 | **10 players** | TPS 19.7, mean 45 ms, p95 63 ms (2 GB heap, before tuning). |
| 9 | **25 players** | A25 defaults: mean 94 ms, p95 163, p99 235. B25 tuned: mean 81 ms, p95 137, p99 231, about TPS 13. |
| 10 | **50 players** | Best config, F50 (vd 6 / sd 4 plus entity tuning): **mean 92 ms, p50 86, p95 139, p99 213, max 920 ms, TPS 11.8**. The other configs were worse (B50, C50, D50, E50 and G50 in WORLD_50.md). |
| 11 | **Worst tick** | F50 920 ms. A 12.4 s watchdog stall in G50 came from `addPluginChunkTicket` loading hall chunks synchronously; it is **fixed** (async preparation, tickets on loaded chunks only). |
| 12 | **p95** | 50 players: 139 ms (F50), down from 308 ms (B50, vd 8 / sd 5). |
| 13 | **p99** | 50 players: 213 ms (F50), down from 451 ms (B50). |
| 14 | **Profiler findings** | JFR main-thread profiles (`settings=profile`) stand in for spark, whose viewer needs an upload that is blocked here. Vanilla dominates: chunk ticking (random ticks and spawning) about 25 %, mob AI and pathfinding about 20 %, entity tracking about 5 %. SÜLD's own instrumented code is 3–7 ms per tick at 25–50 players (about 5 %). The HUD event redraws were the largest part and are now capped at one per player per 2 ticks. |
| 15 | **Memory** | 90-minute soak with 25 mixed bots and 10 churn bots (1 838 join/quit cycles, 1 903 teleports, 174 dungeon entries). The heap after a full GC goes from 777 MB to a plateau of about 2.2 GB under load, with no upward trend. It returns to **1.05 GB** 30 s after the last bot leaves. Threads go from 116 to 127, so there is no leak. |
| 16 | **GC** | G1 with Aikar flags, 4 GB heap. GC total 3.0 s in the F50 run (10.7 s in B50). Young pauses are 30–170 ms on this bench because the GC threads compete with the bots. |
| 17 | **Bottlenecks** | (a) Single-thread CPU: vanilla entity and chunk ticking at 50 spread-out players. (b) Entity count from generated terrain: monsters, items, persistent animals. (c) The bench itself: 4 shared cores with the load generator on the same machine. Ambient and water mobs pile up in chunks that are loaded but not ticking (view 6 > simulation 4); this is not a leak, and the remedy is in point 21. |
| 18 | **Fixes** | Spawn and despawn tuning (25 players: mean −14 %); vd 6 / sd 4 (p95 −31 %); entity density (F50 vs G50: mean −24 %, p95 −25 %, p99 −31 %); async hall chunks (the 12.4 s stall is gone); HUD redraw cap; every SÜLD teleport through `teleportAsync` (`perf/SafeTeleport`); the ovoo is built only in fully loaded chunks; pregen MSPT throttle. |
| 19 | **Async teleports** | Every SÜLD teleport (spawn, relay, dungeon gates and halls, area travel) goes through `SafeTeleport`, which wraps `teleportAsync`. `/suldperf` showed no synchronous chunk load from SÜLD. |
| 20 | **Files** | World: `worldbuild/WorldBorderService`, `PregenService`, `SuldWorldCommand`, suld-api `worldbuild/BorderSpec`, `PregenPlan`. Performance: `perf/SafeTeleport`, `perf/PerfService`. Deploy: `deploy/paper-tuning.conf`, `deploy/tools/paper_tune.py`, `deploy/deploy.sh` (`tune_paper`), `deploy/templates/server.properties.tpl`. Docs: `docs/perf/WORLD_50.md`, `docs/world/*.md`. Gameplay (same night): dungeon halls and ladder, chat, lock-on, tutorial, NPC dialogue, ovoo; see the commit log. (The Naadam minigames were added and later removed on the owner's decision.) |
| 21 | **Remaining tuning option (not applied)** | `chunks.entity-per-chunk-save-limit` for bats and nautilus, if a long-running production world shows the ambient pile-up. `paper_tune.py` needs a small extension to add new map keys. |
| 22 | **Tests** | The full unit-test suites pass, including new ones (`PregenPlanTest`, `OvooCircleTest`, `ChatHistoryTest`, `TutorialProgressTest`, …). Bots on the dev Paper: functional bot 16/16; the ovoo and steppe-horse checks (including the saddle lock); 10/25/50 benchmarks and the 90-minute soak. |
| 23 | **Functional safety (same night)** | Two independent code reviews led to 20 gameplay fixes: exploits, dupes, relog resets, stuck players and main-thread loads. They are listed in PROJECT_STATE.md and the commit log. |
| 24 | **Risks** | (a) Not measured on production hardware. (b) Bots are not real clients: resource pack, visuals and feel are MANUAL_QA_REQUIRED. (c) Real players spread wider than bots, so generation and entity load could be higher; the pre-generator and the generate-rate cap bound it. (d) The ambient pile-up on very long sessions (point 21). (e) Third-party plugins in the profile (EssentialsX, WorldGuard, CoreProtect, …) were present in every run, but their own costs were not tuned. |
| 25 | **Release gate** | ✔ border works, persists and cannot be escaped · ✔ view/simulation benchmark · ✔ chunk generation benchmark · ✔ 10 players · ✘ 25 players (TPS 13) · ✘ 50 players (TPS 11.8) on this bench · ✔ no database IO on the main thread · ✔ profiling (JFR) · ◐ memory soak 90 minutes (2 h+ on the production host still owed) · ✘ real-client QA (owner). |
| 26 | **Recommendation** | Do not open to 50 players on hardware like this bench. Use a dedicated host with high single-thread speed (Ryzen 7000-series or better at about 5 GHz, roughly 2–2.5× this bench), with the load generator on another machine. Keep vd 6 / sd 4 and `deploy/paper-tuning.conf`. Then repeat exactly this benchmark (`bots/mmo50.js`, same mix) and a 2 h+ soak there. Open at 25 players first if p95 is ≤ 50 ms. Raise the view distance to 7–8 only if p95 stays under 40 ms. |
