# Combat feel: lock-on and attack decals

SÜLD's own implementation, server-side only, built on the resource pack. It is inspired by lock-on mods such as
Better Lock On, but no code or assets are copied.

## Lock-on (`CombatFeel`)

| Input (class weapon in hand) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Q** | Lock onto the creature nearest the crosshair: within 24 blocks, inside a 40° cone, in line of sight. Q again releases. |
| **Shift + Q** while locked | Cycle to the next best target. |

* The class weapon can never be dropped (SoulboundGuard), so Q is free as the lock key. In an open container Q
  still means "drop" and is refused as before.
* While locked, the camera **eases** onto the target. Each tick `Player#lookAt` turns it 45 % of the remaining angle,
  and once within 1.5° it holds exactly on the target, at chest height.
* Every aimed skill (beams, volleys, Онгон, Тэнгэрийн Цавчилт), arrow and melee hit therefore goes to the target with
  no extra aim code.
* The HUD target frame shows the locked target first.
* A gold **reticle** (`entity/vfx/reticle`) floats over the target. Only the locking player sees it
  (`setVisibleByDefault(false)` + `showEntity`).
* The lock is released when:
  * the target dies;
  * it is more than 32 blocks away;
  * it has been out of sight for 2 s;
  * the player changes world, quits or dies.
* The lock never targets players, city NPCs (mannequins), tamed animals or armour stands.

## Attack decals

A full-strength swing (attack cooldown ≥ 90 %) with the class weapon draws the class's motion as **one** short-lived
`ItemDisplay`. The client interpolates its turn and growth over 4 ticks, and the decal is removed after 5–6 ticks.

| Class | Decal | Colour | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Баатар | `slash_h` | `#FF5A46` war red | horizontal sweep −55° → +55°, scale 2.4 → 2.9 |
| Дархан | `slash_v` | `#FF9A3C` forge ember | overhead chop, scale 2.2 → 2.6 |
| Хүлэгчин | `thrust` | `#DDE6F0` steel | straight lance thrust, stretching 1.4 blocks forward |
| Бөө | `slash_h` | `#AA6EFF` spirit violet | reverse sweep +50° → −50° |
| Мэргэн | `ring` | `#F2D27A` gold | release flash at the bow on a full draw (≥ 90 % force) |

**Cost:** at most one entity per player per 250 ms. Each decal has no tick task of its own (two delayed tasks), is
not persistent and is limited to a view range of 32 blocks. Leftover `suld_vfx` displays are swept on start.

**Assets:** generated by `tools/pack/gen_vfx.py` (drawn from scratch: 32×32 white textures with alpha, tinted per
class through `custom_model_data` colours).

## Сөхрөх: the dodge (`DodgeService`)

* **Input:** jump while sneaking. The player dashes about 4 blocks in the direction they are moving. Standing still,
  they dash a step back from where they look.
* **Immunity:** for 0.4 s, hits from mobs, bosses and their abilities miss (sparkles show the miss). The void, /kill,
  starvation and the fall at the end still count.
* **Cooldown:** 3 s, or 2 s for the Хүлэгчин (light cavalry). The action bar shows the time left when it is not ready.
* **Not available:** mounted, gliding, flying, in water, or as a soul.

This is how a telegraphed slam or nova (the red ground rings, `ArchetypeBrain`) is escaped at the last moment.

Bot check on the dev Paper: a sneak-jump fires the dodge, and the server sends the dash velocity (1.05 blocks/tick
horizontal). The test bot does not apply its own velocity while sneaking. MANUAL_QA_REQUIRED in a real client: the
dash distance, and dodging a boss slam.

## QA

* **Verified with bots:** the lock key does not drop the weapon; lock and release; decals are spawned and cleaned up.
* **MANUAL_QA_REQUIRED in a real client:**
  * that the camera glide feels right, and is not too strong;
  * that the reticle is visible and correctly placed;
  * that each class's decal reads as its weapon motion.
